## What is Infuse 8 PRO?

Infuse is a video player for macOS from Firecore, LLC. It plays video you already own — on your Mac, on a PC or NAS, on a Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin server, or in cloud storage — and builds a library around it with posters, cast, ratings, and trailers pulled from TMDB. It carries no content of its own and opens no streaming catalogues.

One point deserves stating plainly: no separate paid app called "Infuse 8 Pro" exists in the Mac App Store. There is one free Infuse download, and Pro is an in-app purchase unlocking extra formats and features. "Infuse 8 PRO" means the 8.x branch with a Pro licence active.

## Containers and codecs

What Infuse opens without conversion is why most people install it. Containers: MKV, MP4, MOV, AVI, TS and M2TS, WMV, WebM, ISO and IMG disc images, VIDEO_TS folders, and BDMV Blu-ray structures. Codecs: H.264 (AVC), H.265 (HEVC), H.266 (VVC), AV1, VP9, and VC1.

Playback of 4K Ultra HD is hardware accelerated: Firecore says decoding on Apple silicon keeps 4K HDR smooth at minimal battery cost. HDR10, HDR10+, and HLG are supported, and Dolby Vision (Profiles 5 and 8) comes with Pro. The vendor qualifies all of it with "on compatible hardware": your display and receiver decide what you actually get, and a Mac's built-in screen will not carry every mode.

## Audio tracks and passthrough

Ordinary tracks — AAC, AC3, E-AC3, DTS, FLAC, PCM, OGG, TTA, WMA — play in the free edition. Pro adds high-resolution Dolby and DTS-HD audio, Dolby Atmos (E-AC3) passthrough to a compatible receiver, and AirPlay and Google Cast that carry surround sound and subtitles to a TV.

## Subtitles and metadata

Infuse reads your own subtitle files and pulls new ones from OpenSubtitles in a click: SRT, SSA/ASS, PGS, SUP, VobSub, VTT, SMI, MicroDVD and more, image-based and text-based alike. Library data comes from TMDB, with filters by genre, rating, resolution, and watched status. Intro and credit skipping runs on IntroDB.app data, and Trakt scrobbling logs what you watch.

## Network sources and media servers

Infuse reaches a Mac, PC, or NAS directly over SMB, NFS, FTP, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV, and UPnP/DLNA, with no server software in between. It is also a full client for Plex, Emby, and Jellyfin, syncing watch history and ratings back; release 8.5 added server-side transcoding controls for links that cannot carry the original stream. Pro extends the same browsing to Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, and pCloud.

## One library across Apple platforms

Infuse is not a single Mac program but one app across iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac, and Vision. iCloud sync carries the library, settings, and server connections between them; with Pro, playback progress and history travel too, so a film paused on Apple TV resumes at the same second on the Mac. One Pro purchase covers every platform and supports Family Sharing.
